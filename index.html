04 — The difference

We build the version that holds up.

Modern building science isn't a finish. It's how we plan, sequence, and detail the work — so the building performs the way it should once you're living or working in it. These decisions matter most before framing closes — which is why they shape every custom home and ground-up commercial build we touch.

i. Pre-framing · Pre-design

Site & envelope planning

Drainage, sun path, exterior assemblies, air-barrier continuity, blower-door target.

"We make decisions before you can't fix them anymore."
ii. All stages

Moisture control

Air sealing, drainage planes, vapor management, capillary breaks.

"Your build won't grow mold in five years."
iii. Pre-mechanical

Airflow & IAQ

Mechanical ventilation, balanced air, IAQ-aware finishes, low-VOC specs.

"Rooms feel right — not stuffy, not chemical."
iv. Pre-finish

Durable assemblies

Detail at transitions, flashing, fastener and material compatibility.

"What's behind the drywall lasts as long as the finish."
v. Spec stage

Maintainable finishes

Wear-rated finishes, repairability, real-world cleaning, replacement parts.

"You're not redoing this in seven years."

06 — Founder

Built by Shane Miller, since 2015.

Shane is a South Carolina native who spent his early career in marketing and sales before moving full-time into construction. He started Shane Built Co in 2015 to build the kind of work he kept seeing missing in the Midlands — the project where the planning behind the finish was as carefully thought-through as the finish itself.

He's still on every project. Site walks, scope decisions, framing reviews on custom homes, and the punch list at the end — that's him, not a sales rep handing off to a foreman.
